Privacy Policy

FantasyBrain gives fantasy sports managers analysis of their own leagues. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. In short: we use your league data only to advise you, we request read-only access, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a password stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Fantasy league data: for leagues you choose to connect, the league's settings, standings, rosters, lineups, matchups, free agents, transactions and player statistics, as provided by the fantasy platform.
  • Platform access tokens: when you connect a fantasy platform account, the read-only access tokens it issues. We store them encrypted and use them only to refresh leagues you've imported.
  • Your decisions: the recommendations you accept, override or defer in your decision journal, and any reasons you write down.
  • Usage counts: how many questions you've asked the assistant each day, to apply plan limits.
  • Purchases: which pass you bought and when. Card details are handled by our payment processor, Stripe, and never reach us.

We don't store the names, user IDs or emails of the other managers in your leagues; we keep team names and fantasy data only. Conversations with the assistant are kept in your browser for the current session and aren't stored on our servers.

How we use it

  • To produce the analysis you see: lineup advice, matchup odds, waiver and trade ideas, and answers to your questions about your league.
  • To keep imported leagues current, when you open them or ask for a refresh.
  • To run your account, apply your plan, and keep the service secure.

We never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build advertising profiles. League data is shown only to the manager who connected it.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the service, and only what each needs:

  • AI model provider: when you ask the assistant a question, your question and a summary of that league's current state are sent to our AI provider to generate the answer. We don't use your data to train models.
  • Stripe: to process payments.
  • Hosting: the servers and storage that run FantasyBrain.

Fantasy platforms

Connections to fantasy platforms are read-only. FantasyBrain cannot change your lineup, make transactions, or post in your league. You can disconnect a platform at any time from the Leagues page; we then delete its access tokens, and imported leagues stop refreshing. FantasyBrain isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any fantasy platform.

Cookies

We use a cookie to keep you signed in, one to remember your preferred briefing depth, and a short-lived cookie to secure platform sign-ins. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Security and retention

Passwords are hashed, platform tokens are encrypted at rest, and connections use HTTPS. We keep your data while your account is open. You can export everything from your account page, remove any league (with its history and journal), or delete your account, which permanently removes your data.

Children

FantasyBrain is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. Questions or requests: contact us through github.com/fantasybrainproject.
